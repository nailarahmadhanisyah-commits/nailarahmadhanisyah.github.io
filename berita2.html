LNGSHOT Looks Casual on the Cover of DAZED Korea's October Issue

This collaboration is also part of a joint campaign with Labruket and DAZED Korea. The photoshoot, combining fashion, lifestyle, and LNGSHOT's musical characteristics, adds a unique touch to the group's appearance, which is currently establishing its identity in the K-pop industry. LNGSHOT is known for its hip-hop influences and bolder musical approach. Their appearance in fashion magazines like DAZED Korea provides an opportunity to showcase the members' off-stage sides. With a concept that prioritizes comfort and natural style, LNGSHOT proves that looking good doesn't always have to be action-packed. Relaxed, stylish, and ready to "TAKE A SHOT." .
